How Age is Calculated

The age of a person can be counted differently in different cultures. This calculator is based on the most common age system. In this system, age increases on a person's birthday. For example, the age of a person who has lived for 3 years and 11 months is 3, and their age will increase to 4 on their next birthday one month later.

In some cultures, age is expressed by counting years with or without including the current year. For example, one person who is twenty years old is the same as one person who is in the twenty-first year of his/her life.

In one of the traditional Chinese age systems, people are born at age 1 and the age grows up at the Traditional Chinese New Year instead of birthday. For example, if one baby was born just one day before the Traditional Chinese New Year, 2 days later, the baby will be at age 2 even though he/she is only 2 days old.

Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is this age calculator?
This age calculator is highly accurate. It accounts for leap years and calculates the exact number of years, months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, and seconds between two dates using precise date arithmetic.
Can I calculate age for a future date?
Yes! Simply change the "Age at the Date of" field to any future date. The calculator will tell you exactly how old you (or anyone else) will be on that date.
Does the calculator handle leap years?
Yes. The calculator fully accounts for leap years (years divisible by 4, except century years unless divisible by 400), ensuring accurate day counts for all date ranges.
Can I calculate the age of someone who was born before 1900?
Yes. Simply type any four-digit birth year into the year field. The calculator supports any historical date as long as a valid year is entered.
How do I calculate my age in days?
Use this calculator — after clicking Calculate, the result shows your exact age in total days among other units. For example, a 25-year-old person is approximately 9,131 days old.
